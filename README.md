# In memory of John C. Hull

This repository preserves supplementary materials developed by Professor John C. Hull in connection with his teaching and academic work. They are made available so that they may continue to support students, instructors, and researchers.

Except where otherwise indicated, the materials are licensed under the [Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International License (CC BY-NC 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/). Please credit John C. Hull when using or adapting these materials.

This archive is made possible by the generous support of donors to the [John Hull Financial Innovation Fund](https://www.rotman.utoronto.ca/alumni-and-giving/invest-in-rotman/john-hull-financial-innovation-fund/), with support from [Rotman FinHub](https://www.rotman.utoronto.ca/faculty-and-research/research-centres/finhub/), and with permission from John's family. We hope this archive will allow John's work to continue teaching, informing, and inspiring people around the world.
